隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 27 日

我们访问哪些数据

在你授权后，Mail Connector 以只读方式访问你的邮箱，包括邮件的发件人、收件人、主题、时间、摘要和正文，以及你的邮箱地址和账号名称。我们不会发送、删除或修改你的邮件。

如何使用这些数据

数据仅用于你本人通过 AI 助手查询邮件，以及在收到新邮件时提醒你。我们不会出售你的数据，不会将其用于广告，也不会用于训练通用的人工智能模型。

如何存储和保护

授权凭证经过 AES-256 加密后存储。邮件内容按需从邮件服务商实时读取，不做长期保存；新邮件提醒记录最多保留 30 天。

如何撤销授权

你可以随时在 AI 助手中断开邮箱连接，或者在 Google 账号权限页面、Microsoft 账号权限页面 撤销授权。撤销后，相关凭证会被删除。

Google 用户数据

Mail Connector 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的有限使用要求。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Data we access

After you grant permission, Mail Connector has read-only access to your mailbox: sender, recipients, subject, date, snippet and body of messages, plus your email address and account name. We never send, delete or modify your mail.

How we use it

The data is used only so that you can query your own mail through the AI assistant and be notified about new messages. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted with AES-256. Message content is fetched from the mail provider on demand and is not stored long term; new-mail notification records are kept for at most 30 days.

Revoking access

You can disconnect your mailbox in the AI assistant at any time, or revoke access on your Google account permissions page or Microsoft account permissions page. Stored tokens are deleted when you disconnect.

Google user data

Mail Connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
